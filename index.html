The full cycle, handled end to end.

Whether you have no data resource at all, or someone capable who has never built a solution like this before, ClearMetric picks up the parts you need.

  • Data extraction & ETL

    Excel extracts, text files, SQL reports, manually maintained spreadsheets — or pulling the data out of the source system directly. Cleaned, combined and made consistent.

  • Data modelling

    A properly structured model with the right relationships underneath, so reporting stays accurate and quick as more gets added to it.

  • DAX measures & KPIs

    Your business rules written once, calculated the same way every time. One agreed definition per number, so teams stop arriving at meetings with different figures.

  • Power BI dashboards

    Reports designed around the decisions being made, not around the data that happens to be available. Clear enough that nobody needs training to read them.

  • Pipelines & automation

    Scheduled refreshes and automated data flows so the monthly rebuild stops being someone's job. Microsoft Fabric pipelines where the scale justifies them.

  • Review & optimisation

    Already have Power BI, but it's slow, fragile or nobody dares change it? Existing solutions reviewed, repaired and made maintainable.
